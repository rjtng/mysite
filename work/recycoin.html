RECYCOIN

A reverse vending machine for PET bottles. A camera running YOLOv8 checks each bottle, and residents earn points they redeem with barangay personnel.

  • Sees the bottle

    YOLOv8 looks for a bottle in the camera feed and sizes it from its outline: 250 ml, 500 ml, 1 L or 1.5 L, worth 0.5 to 3 points.

  • Hard to cheat

    A bottle has to hold still through at least three of five checks before it counts. A hand in the chute, or a disturbed chute, cancels the deposit.

  • Knows when it’s full

    An ultrasonic sensor reads the fill level of the 3.5 ft bin. At 95% the machine stops accepting bottles and the dashboard shows it.

How a deposit works

A resident opens their RECYCOIN page with their Resident ID and starts a session, which holds the machine for them until they finish.

Each bottle goes into the chute. The camera confirms and sizes it, the two servo gates open, the bottle drops into the bin and the gates close again. The screen on the machine narrates each step — verifying, verified with the points, opening, depositing, closing — and the points land on the resident’s balance.

Redeeming points

Barangay personnel sign in to a page of their own, enter a resident’s ID and redeem points against the balance. Each redemption goes into a transaction history, and the resident’s page updates when their points change.

The hardware

A Raspberry Pi runs the camera and a small Flask service the web app talks to: start and stop the camera, poll for verified bottles, read how full the bin is. The double acceptance gate is a pair of MG996R servos; an IR sensor at the closing gate stops it for a hand in the chute, and an ultrasonic sensor at the top measures the fill.
