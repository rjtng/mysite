ReadySetHire

An interview-practice app for fresh graduates and job seekers: AI mock interviews, aptitude tests and a résumé builder in one Flutter app.

  • AI mock interviews

    Gemini plays an HR interviewer, with follow-ups that get harder as you go and questions shaped by your résumé. You answer out loud, and questions are read back to you.

  • Feedback on the transcript

    When the interview ends, a second pass reads the whole transcript and writes an evidence-based feedback report on your answers.

  • Aptitude tests

    Situational judgement, diagrammatic reasoning and verbal reasoning, with past results kept in a history you can chart.

Who it's for

Fresh graduates and job seekers getting ready for their first interviews, and the schools and career counselors who help them get there.

Inside the app

Your background — basic information, academic history and experience — goes in once. It builds a résumé you can export as a PDF, and it personalises the interview questions.

Around the mock interview sit an interview chatbot, flashcards, and a guide hub with a résumé optimiser, interview tips and advice on what to wear. Accounts run on Firebase: email with verification and password reset, or Google sign-in. A feedback form takes bug reports, feature requests and questions.
