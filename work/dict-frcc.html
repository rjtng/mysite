  • Camera check-in

    Employees check in with a photo. The camera captures on its own after three seconds, with a preview and a retake, and every record keeps the time.

  • Compliance at a glance

    The admin dashboard splits staff into compliant and non-compliant for the latest Monday, filtered by area of assignment and department.

  • Two roles

    Administrators, capped at three accounts, manage the employee list and the records. Employees see their own attendance.

Why it exists

Staff at DICT Region IX and BASULTA are expected at the flag-raising ceremony every Monday. The checker replaces manual tracking with a web system that records who checked in and when, and gives administrators the compliance picture as it happens.

What I built

I built it with two fellow Universidad de Zamboanga interns, Walid B. Salih and Mohammad Salih S. Musa, during our internship with the Management Information Systems Service.

I designed the MySQL schema and the reporting layer — DataTables for the records, Chart.js for the dashboards — and worked on role-based access, employee management and the browser camera capture. Every query goes through PDO prepared statements.

From testing to rollout

The team ran functional testing and QA before rollout, and the system went into production for 50+ employees after HR approved it.

The last weeks added the three-second auto-capture with preview and retake, a list of recent records on the check-in page, a duplicate-username check and the three-administrator cap. Whether a check-in counts as on time, 8:30 a.m., is shown only to administrators.
