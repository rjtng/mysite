03 / Projects & Portfolio

Two worth explaining, then the rest

Most of my public repositories are small. The larger systems I have worked on live on school servers or in private repos, which is why this list is shorter than the credential list below it.

2026 RSH

Personal project · In progress

ReadySetHire

A recruitment app in Flutter covering candidate profiles, job listings and the stages an application moves through. One Dart codebase for Android and iOS, with Firebase behind it.

The interesting part has been state. An application belongs to a candidate and to a role at the same time, and both sides want to see it change. I rewrote that layer twice before it stopped fighting me.

Dart · Flutter · Firebase · Material

Repository
2025 BM

Records system · Local government

Barangay Maganda MIS

A management information system for a barangay office: resident records, document requests, and the reports the staff have to file. PHP and MySQL, deliberately, because it had to run on the hosting they already had.

Building for clerks rather than developers changed how I designed it. Fewer screens, bigger targets, and every destructive action asks twice.

PHP · MySQL · Apache · Bootstrap
