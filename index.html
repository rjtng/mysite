My degree is computer engineering, which meant as much time on logic gates, microprocessors and subnet masks as on software. Everything I know about the web and mobile side, I taught myself alongside it.

In practice that makes me a generalist. I have written PHP and MySQL systems for local government records, Flutter apps that run on Android and iOS from one codebase, and small OpenCV programs that track faces in a live camera feed. The computer vision work is the part I keep coming back to, even when nobody asked for it.

Since the middle of 2024 I have worked through sixty certificate programmes from IBM, Google, Cisco, Meta and Microsoft. That number sounds ridiculous written down and I know how it reads. The honest version is that structured courses are how I learn best when there is no senior developer nearby to ask, and each one ends in a graded project rather than a quiz.

What I want next is a team. I have done a lot of learning alone and I would like to do the next part of it with people who are further along than me.

  • Building ReadySetHire, a recruitment app in Flutter
  • Learning Kubernetes properly, past the skill badges
  • Reading more about real-time tracking than is probably healthy

A recruitment app in Flutter covering candidate profiles, job listings and the stages an application moves through. One Dart codebase for Android and iOS, with Firebase behind it.

The interesting part has been state. An application belongs to a candidate and to a role at the same time, and both sides want to see it change. I rewrote that layer twice before it stopped fighting me.
